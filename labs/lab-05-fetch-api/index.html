About the Tech Event

Welcome to the official portal for the SEU Tech Fest 2026. This event brings together technology enthusiasts, student developers, and innovative minds to demonstrate their skills, collaborate on technical projects, and network with industry leaders.

Our main goal is to promote practical software programming skills and foster innovation among computer science students through hands-on workshops and competitive coding tracks.

Special Announcement: Registration is completely free for all active Southeast University students!

For official university announcements, visit the Southeast University Website.
